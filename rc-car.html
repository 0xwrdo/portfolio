21V RC Car

Rebuilt from scratch on a BTS7960 motor driver and FlySky FS-i6 radio system — 21V pack, ~2km range, switchable power modes.

ArduinoBTS7960FlySky FS-i621V~2km Range

Specs

  • Motor DriverBTS7960 dual H-bridge — handles high current without burning up.
  • RadioFlySky FS-i6 6-channel transmitter / FS-iA6B receiver. ~2km reliable range.
  • Power21V LiPo pack with switchable power modes via Arduino.
  • ControlCustom Arduino sketch handling throttle mixing, power mode switching, and failsafe.
