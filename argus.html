ARGUS Trekking Safety

A wearable vitals monitor and web portal system for trekker safety and emergency response — built with a team of four at the Build Nepal Hackathon 2026.

ESP32MAX30102Next.jsSupabaseAug 2026

What it does

🫀

Wristband

ESP32 with MAX30102 sensor for heart rate and SpO2, paired to a phone over Bluetooth.

📍

Location & Alerts

Phone GPS for positioning, WhatsApp alerts when a trekker needs help.

🛡️

Dual Portals

Next.js/Supabase backend with separate portals for trekkers and monitoring authorities.
